Methodology

  • Universe — current constituents of the S&P 500, S&P MidCap 400 and S&P SmallCap 600 (together the S&P Composite 1500), taken from the Wikipedia constituent lists.
  • Prices — daily dividend- and split-adjusted closes, roughly 13 months of history per security.
  • 12‑1 month return — price 21 trading days ago divided by price 252 trading days ago, minus 1. The most recent month is excluded, following the standard momentum construction.
  • 6‑1 month return — price 21 trading days ago divided by price 126 trading days ago, minus 1.
  • Volatility — sample standard deviation of daily adjusted returns over the trailing 252 (12m) or 126 (6m) trading days, annualized: σann = std(daily returns) × √252.
  • Scores — score_12 = return_12_1 / volatility_12m, score_6 = return_6_1 / volatility_6m, and the primary ranking metric final_score = 0.50 × score_12 + 0.50 × score_6. An alternative blend is retained: alt_score = ((return_12_1 + return_6_1)/2) / ((volatility_12m + volatility_6m)/2).
  • Ranks — rank 1 is the highest final_score. Securities are ranked within their own index and across the full S&P 1500. Securities without a complete 12-month history (recent IPOs, new index members) are excluded and listed in the data file.

About

What this is

All S&P 1500 stocks (the S&P 500, MidCap 400 and SmallCap 600 together), ranked by risk-adjusted momentum — best first. Updated automatically every trading night.

How the numbers work

12-1 / 6-1 return — how much the stock moved over the past 12 or 6 months, skipping the most recent month.

Volatility — how bumpy the ride was, from daily price swings, annualized.

VAR (volatility-adjusted return) — return divided by volatility: reward per unit of risk. The list is ordered by Avg. VAR, the average of the 12-1 and 6-1 VARs.
